Privacy Policy

Information on personal data processing when browsing the Grimaldelli website or contacting the company, pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679.

Data controller

The data controller is Grimaldelli S.r.l., Strada Provinciale 20, 26010 Salvirola (CR), Italy, VAT no. 01190000198. For data protection enquiries and requests, please email privacy@glcgrimaldelli.com.

Browsing data

Accessing the website transmits technical information needed for communication, including IP address, request date and time, visited resources and browser or device details. The hosting provider may process this information to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

The current website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Technical provider logs are separate from the content of messages sent to the company.

Enquiries and job applications

The website provides telephone numbers and email links. There are no active web forms or CV uploads: messages are sent only if you choose to use your email application.

If you write to us, we process the information in your message, such as your name, contact details, enquiry or CV, to answer your request or assess a spontaneous application. Please avoid sending special-category information that is not relevant to your enquiry.

Purposes and legal grounds

Business enquiries and unsolicited applications are handled to respond and to take appropriate pre-contractual steps, where applicable under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Technical security and abuse prevention may rely on the controller's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Relevant legal obligations also apply.

Recipients and retention

Information is made available to authorised employees and, when necessary, IT and email service providers. Technical browsing data may be processed by the hosting provider. Any international data transfers depend on the services involved and must comply with the GDPR safeguards.

Personal information is retained only for the period needed to handle enquiries, applications and applicable obligations. For information on criteria relating to your specific request, contact the data controller using the privacy address above.

Your rights

Where applicable under GDPR Articles 15–22, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing by contacting privacy@glcgrimaldelli.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.

Cookies and external links

The current website does not use advertising or profiling cookies or visitor analytics in the verified configuration. See the Cookie Policy for more information. Social networks and other external websites are accessed only when you open their links.
