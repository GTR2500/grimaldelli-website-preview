Cookie Policy

Information on cookies and tracking technologies used on the Grimaldelli website.

Cookies on this website

The website consists of static pages and resources. Technical checks of the published version detected no profiling or analytics cookies, advertising pixels or tracking identifiers set by the website code. External video and social content is not embedded or loaded automatically.

No prior consent to non-essential cookies is required for the configuration assessed, and no consent banner is displayed.

Technical cookie for form security

When a contact or job application form is used on the company domain, the website may create the technical session cookie grimaldelli_forms. It protects submissions against unauthorised requests (CSRF token). It lasts only for the browser session, uses Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, and is not used for analytics or profiling. Prior consent is not required because it is strictly necessary for the requested service.

Technical browsing information

The website hosting provider may process connection data and technical logs required for service delivery, security and diagnostics. Further details are provided in the Privacy Policy.

Project photographs and videos

Project photographs and videos are stored as website assets and are played directly from the Grimaldelli domain, without Instagram embeds or automatic connections to Meta. The galleries do not require additional cookies. Links to social profiles elsewhere on the website remain external and open only if selected by the visitor.

Links to external websites

The website includes links to social networks, messaging services and other sources. If you follow an external link, the privacy and cookie practices of that third-party provider apply.

Policy updates

This policy is updated when the technologies used by the website change. If non-essential tracking is introduced in the future, prior consent controls will be provided where required by law.
